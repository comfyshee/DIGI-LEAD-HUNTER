## 🖱️ Running Your First Lead Hunt

Once the app is open, you will see a simple working area. Here is the flow in plain terms:

1. **Choose your category.** Type what kind of business you want to find — for example, "dentists", "plumbers", "cafes", or "accountants". The more specific you are, the more useful your results will be.
2. **Choose your location.** Type the town, city, or area you want to search in. Local selling works best when you stay close to home at first.
3. **Set how many leads you want.** Start small. A first run of a modest number is a good way to see how the app behaves and how long a search takes on your connection.
4. **Start the search.** Click the start button and let the app work. You can leave it running in the background and check back in a few minutes.
5. **Watch the progress.** The app shows you what it is doing as it goes — how many leads it has collected and whether it is still working.
6. **Save your results.** When it finishes, save your list somewhere you will find it later, such as your Desktop or Documents folder.

Repeat the process for each category and area you care about. Most people build up their list over a few short sessions rather than all at once.

## 📊 Understanding Your Results

The app gives you two things.

**The lead list.** This is your prospect data — the businesses that matched what you asked for. Treat it as a working document. Open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet program. Add your own columns: "called", "emailed", "not interested", "call back next month". The list becomes more valuable the more you mark it up.

**The blueprint.** This is the planning document the app generates alongside your list. It gives you a starting structure for outreach — who to contact first, what to say, and how to keep track of the conversation. Think of it as a suggested route rather than a strict rulebook. Adapt it to how you actually sell.

A good habit: work through your list in small batches. Twenty well-researched conversations beat two hundred ignored emails.

## 🛡️ Keeping Your Data Safe

Your lead lists are business information and worth protecting.

- Store your results in a folder you back up, such as OneDrive, Google Drive, or an external drive.
- Keep one master copy of each list and work from copies, so a mistake never costs you the original.
- Do not share your working files publicly. They are your commercial research.
- Only collect and contact businesses in ways that respect local rules on business communication. If you are unsure, check the guidance from your local business authority before starting outreach.
